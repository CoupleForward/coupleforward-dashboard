# REWIRE Lab → Video Library Shoot List (planning, fill-in-ready)

Companion to `01-journey-architecture.md`. The library exists to guide a couple (or individual) along each node of the journey: Christian on camera is the guide who meets them at every destination. ## The per-node video kit (the repeating unit)

Every node gets the same four-video kit. Uniformity keeps the shoot efficient (batchable by kit type, not by node) and makes the library legible to members.

| # | Kit video | Job | Target length |
|---|---|---|---|
| V1 | **Arrival** | Welcome to this node as a PLACE: what life looks like here, why the journey brought you here now, what the one skill is. Names the enemy of this node (the way people stall here) | 2-4 min |
| V2 | **The Skill** | The teaching itself: the map, the mechanism, the move. Skill AND insight. This is the one that must be Advanced Relationship Calculus, not Psychology 101 | 6-12 min |
| V3 | **The Practice** | Walkthrough of the station tool + the between-Huddle practice: exactly what to do this week, shown, not described. Screen-capture of the station app where one triggers | 3-6 min |
| V4 | **Movement** | How you will know it moved: the node's movement definition in the member's own language, what the system will ask them, and what "finished but unmoved" feels like (named honestly) | 2-3 min |

Register (from the canon): clinical precision, never rally-cry. "Your body decided you were in danger before your brain caught up," not amygdala talk. No healing-journey vocabulary. Direct address, one person at a time, because the viewer is usually one person watching alone.

Solo variants: where a node's solo path differs materially (Nodes 6-7 especially), V3 gets a solo alternate cut. Assume +1 video on roughly a third of nodes.

## Counts and the MVP subset

Full library at a 10-node map: 10 kits × 4 + ~3 solo alts + 8 journey-level ≈ **51 videos**. That is a season of shooting, not a prerequisite.

**Phase 1 (rhythm launch) needs only the R set**: the uploaded Huddle course + R2 + three app orientations + R6 ≈ **5 short new videos plus one upload**, a single light shoot day.

**MVP journey shoot (Phase 3-4) is next**: kits for the three MVP nodes (12 videos) + J1, J2 + J4 (first phase gateway) + any J3 bridge = **~16 videos, roughly two batched shoot days** once scripts exist. Everything else shoots after real travelers exist, which also means later videos get written knowing where pilots actually stalled.

## Production notes (decisions, not gear specs)

→ **The map is the set.** Every video anchors to the same node-map visual (see `04-animated-sales-video.md`): V1s open on the map zooming to this node; phase gateways travel the path. One design artifact, reused everywhere, so product UI, library, and sales video are visibly the same world.
→ **Batch by kit type**: shoot all V1s in a session, all V2s in sessions per phase. Same setup, same energy, massive efficiency.
→ **Talking head + overlay** is enough. The teaching carries it; production value goes into the map animations, which are reused, not per-video spend.
→ Hosting decision (Mux / Vimeo / YouTube unlisted) is open (master doc Q8) and only blocks Phase 4, not scripting.
→ Scripts get drafted from the canon docs per node, then christianized; V2 scripts are the ones worth his heaviest edit, since they are the Calculus-402 promise.
